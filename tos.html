MUTUAL TRUST COVENANT

Agreement of Collaboration

EFFECTIVE DATE: MARCH 29, 2026 | COOPERATIVE REVISION 26-D

1.0 Purpose & Operational Scope

This covenant defines the operational framework between our distinguished charterers, personal assistants, brokerage houses, and certified aircraft operating partners (the "Operators"). 15D Wings™ is a high-fidelity flight-coordination and safety-assurance infrastructure operated under the corporate registry of Sky Party LTD (RC 8997727), registered under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

Service Role: 15D Wings™ is not a direct air carrier. The platform acts as a coordination and dispatch support system, providing flight tracking, data verification, and secure financial processing. All physical flights are executed with absolute authority by direct air carriers holding valid, active Air Operator Certificates (AOC) issued by regional civil aviation authorities (such as the NCAA).

2.0 Operational Standards & Flight Execution

To maintain a flawless safety standard, flights coordinated through the network require verified compliance across core operational criteria, including active landing/overflight permits, mechanical aircraft positioning, certified flight crew readiness, fuel arrangement confirmations, and verified capital allocation.

3.0 Client & Personal Assistant Responsibilities

3.1 Manifest Integrity & Document Validity

Clients and authorized agents must submit accurate passenger manifest details. Names must match passport/ID data exactly as displayed on official travel documents to comply with local aviation security protocols. Passports must remain valid for the required statutory period beyond arrival dates.

3.2 Sourcing Activation Fee, Platform Fees & Cancellations

Voluntary client-side cancellations are governed strictly by the assigned aircraft operator’s terms and conditions. 15D Wings™ does not issue or retain flight credits as a standard practice; however, if a client explicitly insists and elects to retain funds as a Flight Credit at their own discretion, it may be accommodated within our operator network.

The Sourcing Activation Payment covers initial route validation, aircraft sourcing, and quotation coordination. Once a valid operator quotation meeting disclosed requirements is presented, the Sourcing Activation Payment becomes the earned sourcing fee. If a suitable aircraft cannot be sourced, or if the client withdraws prior to quotation delivery, the activation payment is refunded, less authorized non-refundable third-party expenses.

3.3 Refund Processing & Financial Audits

All cash reimbursements pass through internal legal and financial compliance audits prior to disbursement. Once approved, refunds are processed directly to the original payment channel within 3 to 5 business days (or up to 7 to 14 business days for complex cross-border or banking reconciliation cases).

4.0 Capital Security & Mission Assurance

To ensure financial transparency and protect operating partners and clients, 15D Wings™ utilizes secure financial processing and maintains the 15D Wings™ Mission Contingency Fund to support mission readiness, positioning, and execution oversight. When a mission is confirmed, operator payouts and contingency fund allocations are handled through secure financial channels, with transparent post-mission reporting and reconciliation for unutilized balances.

5.0 Operator & Intermediary Protocols

Certified operators and registered brokers must maintain verified communications, accurate compliance records, and adhere to established scheduling and milestone windows. Prompt communication of operational updates ensures uninterrupted service delivery across the network.

6.0 Limits of Liability

Under no circumstances shall Sky Party LTD, 15D Wings™, or their respective directors, crew, or agents be held liable for third-party operator failures, meteorological delays, physical runway restrictions, or regional slot closures beyond platform control.

DATA SECURITY PROTOCOL

Privacy Policy & Secure Data Manifesto

LAST UPDATED: MARCH 29, 2026 | NDPR & GDPR COMPLIANT SECURITY

1.0 Data Protection Framework & Jurisdiction

Because premium aviation manifests require sensitive Passenger Identifiable Information (PII), our data pipelines are engineered to meet the strict legal constraints of the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and national security directives governing global Advance Passenger Information Systems (APIS).

2.0 Types of Data Captured

To fulfill border control clearances, immigration checklists, and manifest generations, the platform securely handles standard identification parameters, passport details, APIS metrics, health declarations, and luggage/cargo characteristics.

3.0 Strict Digital Security Mandates

ANTI-PLAINTEXT TRANSMISSION BAN: Passenger passport details and flight manifests must never be sent via unencrypted channels. Secure portals and encrypted pipelines are enforced.

4.0 Third-Party Sharing Rules

Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know operational basis with certified civil aviation regulators, direct aircraft flight crews, and authorized ground handling agents for customs and clearance purposes.

5.0 Storage Retention & Deletion Rights

Manifest data uploaded is automatically managed and deleted or anonymized following standard retention cycles, save for mandatory tax or regulatory archives. Requests for data expungement can be directed to ops@15dwings.com.ng.
