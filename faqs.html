HOW WE WORK,
BOOKING RULES & REFUND POLICY

Clear, honest rules for booking a private flight with 15D Wings™. No hidden clauses, no confusing legal words—just straightforward facts on how we source your aircraft, protect your trip, and refund your money.

SECTION 1 — HOW BOOKING WITH US WORKS

1. OUR ROLE &
HOW WE SOURCE YOUR JET

What 15D Wings™ Does: 15D Wings™ (a trading name of Sky Party Ltd.) is a private flight coordination and booking platform. We do not own aircraft directly; instead, we connect you with licensed, government-approved private jet operators and manage every step of your trip from start to finish.

How We Check Your Trip First: Before you commit to a full flight payment, our system checks flight routes, airport rules, landing permits, aircraft availability, and timing. If a request is unsafe or impossible to operate on time, we tell you upfront so you never waste time or money.

What Happens If One Operator Says No: If the first aircraft operator declines your flight or cannot meet your schedule, you do not need to panic. Our system automatically switches to the next qualified aircraft operator in our network to secure your flight.

THE 48-HOUR LOCK

Once you accept a final price and pay in full—especially within 48 hours of departure—your aircraft, flight crew, fuel, and airport landing permits are permanently locked in for your trip.

SECTION 2 — FEES & FULL REFUND GUARANTEE

2. FEES, COMMITMENTS
& WHEN YOU GET YOUR MONEY BACK

1. Platform Fee (10%): When you ask for a flight, we give you an estimated price range. Our Platform Fee is 10% of the middle point of that estimate. This fee is separate from the aircraft owner's charter price and is shown to you clearly before you pay.

2. Sourcing Activation Payment (Commitment Fee): To start searching for your aircraft and locking in real quotes from operators, you pay 10% of our Platform Fee upfront. This is not an extra charge—if you go ahead with the flight, this deposit is deducted from your total Platform Fee.

3. Mission Contingency Reserve: Every confirmed flight includes a small safety reserve fund used only if an emergency backup plane or last-minute ground support is needed during your trip. If your flight goes smoothly and we do not touch this reserve fund, we refund 100% of the unused reserve money back to you within 1 to 5 business days after your flight lands.

SECTION 3 — SUB-72 HOUR POLICY (URGENT & LAST-MINUTE FLIGHTS)

3. SUB-72 HOUR
URGENT BOOKING POLICY

Applies to all flight requests made less than 72 hours (3 days) before takeoff.

When you book a private flight with less than 72 hours' notice, our team has to move immediately to check live aircraft positions, crew duty hours, fuel availability, and airport permits. Because urgent flights work differently from advance bookings, they are divided into two clear lanes:

LANE A: 24 TO 72 HOURS BEFORE DEPARTURE

1. Quick Decision Window (2 Hours): Because aircraft availability changes every minute on short notice, once we send you a valid aircraft quote within your budget, you have 2 hours to accept or reject it. If you reject the quote within 2 hours, your activation commitment is refunded (minus bank/FX charges).

2. Full Commitment Protection: If we cannot source a suitable aircraft for your 24–72 hour trip, 100% of your commitment payment is refunded.

3. Last-Minute Operator Cancellation Protection (24–48 Hour Window): Once you accept a 24–48 hour quote and confirm the flight, our 180-Minute Backup Guarantee is active. If the aircraft owner cancels last minute or the plane develops a technical fault before takeoff, we will deploy an equivalent replacement aircraft within 180 minutes (3 hours). If we cannot get a replacement plane within 180 minutes, you receive a 100% cash refund of your Mission Contingency Fund and all unused charter money.

LANE B: SAME-DAY FLIGHTS (UNDER 24 HOURS BEFORE DEPARTURE)

Same-day flights have zero room for delay and require immediate emergency work from our dispatch team.

  • Compulsory $160 Flat Same-Day Sourcing Fee: Every same-day flight request requires an upfront flat fee of $160 (or the exact Naira equivalent at the current market rate) before we begin calling operators.
  • Strictly Non-Refundable: This $160 fee is earned immediately the moment we start working on your request. It is non-refundable whether we find an available plane or not. It pays for emergency operator calls, live crew checks, airport night-curfew checks, and airspace clearance verification.
  • Honest Written Report If No Jet Is Available: Even if every aircraft is booked or an airport is closed, we will never leave you guessing. You will receive a written operational report showing the exact reason (for example, airport runway closure, destination sunset curfew, or zero crew availability).
  • Moving to Your Actual Flight: This $160 fee is separate from your aircraft charter price. Once we find your plane and you pay for the flight, all our normal backup and refund protections apply.
SECTION 4 — BACKUP AIRCRAFT GUARANTEE & FORCE MAJEURE

4. BACKUP JETS &
FORCE MAJEURE EXCEPTIONS

OUR 180-MINUTE BACKUP GUARANTEE

If your assigned aircraft develops a mechanical problem (Aircraft On Ground / AOG) or the operator cancels after your flight is confirmed, 15D Wings™ automatically triggers our emergency backup plan to provide a similar replacement aircraft within 180 minutes (3 hours).

If We Fail to Provide a Backup Plane: If a technical breakdown happens and we cannot deploy a replacement aircraft within 180 minutes, we refund 100% of your Mission Contingency Reserve plus all unutilized flight funds straight back to your account.

FORCE MAJEURE (EVENTS BEYOND HUMAN CONTROL THAT GROUND ALL AIRCRAFT)

Our 180-minute replacement guarantee applies to operator and aircraft issues. However, 15D Wings™ cannot be held responsible for delays or cancellations caused by Force Majeure—extreme events outside our control that legally or physically ground all planes. Force Majeure includes:

  • Severe Weather & Natural Disasters: Harmattan dust storms with zero visibility, heavy thunderstorms, flooding, hurricanes, volcanic ash, or earthquakes that make flying unsafe.
  • Government & Aviation Authority Shutdowns: Sudden airspace closures, runway shutdowns, or flight bans ordered by NCAA, NAMA, FAAN, or foreign aviation authorities.
  • War, Civil Unrest & Security Emergencies: Armed conflict, terrorism, riots, curfews, strikes, or sudden VIP/presidential airspace lockdowns that halt airport operations.
  • Airport Infrastructure & National Fuel Crises: Sudden airport power outages, air traffic control radar failures, or nationwide aviation fuel (Jet A-1) shortages affecting all operators at an airport.
  • Public Health Emergencies: Sudden pandemics, quarantine orders, or government border closures.

When a Force Majeure event grounds all aircraft, we will work with the operator to reschedule your flight as soon as the skies reopen, or process a refund of unutilized funds in line with operator and regulatory rules.

SECTION 5 — REFUND TIMELINES, CURRENCIES & BANK ACCOUNTS

5. HOW FAST WE
REFUND YOUR MONEY

Whenever you are owed a refund—whether because we could not find an aircraft, you declined a quote within your allowed time, or your flight finished without using the contingency reserve—here is exactly how your money is returned:

SAME-DAY (WITHIN 24 HOURS)

Refunds paid in Stablecoins (USDT or USDC) are sent to your verified crypto wallet within 24 hours of approval.

1 TO 5 BUSINESS DAYS

Refunds paid via bank transfer—Naira (NGN), US Dollars (USD) wire, or ACH—arrive within 1 to 5 business days.

Currencies Available (Direct Clients): If you booked directly for yourself or your company, you can receive your refund in Naira (NGN) (converted at the live market exchange rate on the day of payout), US Dollars (USD) via bank wire/ACH, or USDT/USDC.

Bank & Exchange Charges: In all refund payouts, standard third-party bank transfer fees, payment gateway fees, and foreign exchange (FX) conversion charges are deducted from the refunded amount.

Where Your Refund Goes: During sign-up (onboarding), the person or company booking the flight must provide a verified bank account or wallet address. All refunds are paid strictly back into that exact registered account.

Legal & Banking Compliance: All Naira, USD, and stablecoin payouts follow Nigerian and international banking laws, including rules set by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), NFIU, SEC, and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations.

Need Help With a Refund? If any approved refund takes longer than 5 business days, email our operations team immediately at ops@15dwings.com.ng so we can resolve it right away.
