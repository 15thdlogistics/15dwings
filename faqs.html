I. MISSION ADMISSION & FEASIBILITY

HOW DOES THE
PROCESS BEGIN?

Every journey begins inside our planning system. Once we receive your request, our decision engines evaluate routing, aircraft suitability, timing sensitivity, regulatory requirements, and overall feasibility.

This allows us to confirm — before anything is committed — that your mission can be executed smoothly. If something introduces avoidable risk, we’ll let you know early and suggest adjustments.

Our goal: If we accept it, we can deliver it.

WHY MIGHT A
REQUEST BE DECLINED?

Occasionally, regulatory restrictions, aircraft positioning realities, or compressed timelines fall outside our execution standards.

When that happens, we will either recommend an alternative or respectfully decline. We only move forward when we’re confident in the outcome.

II. 48-HOUR CONFIRMATION

THE 48-HOUR
COMMITMENT

Around 48 hours prior to departure, your mission shifts from planning mode to commitment mode. This is the "Lock" phase.

/ Aircraft are Secured
/ Crew Finalized
/ Fueling & Handling
/ Permits Activated

Cleared funds allow our system to lock these elements in place and protect your departure window.

III. CREDITS, CANCELLATIONS & CONTINUITY

THE CONTINUITY
STRUCTURE

If you choose to cancel or adjust your mission, funds are typically preserved as a Flight Credit. This keeps your access active within our operator network.

Voluntary Changes

Funds remain as flight credit. Cash reimbursement is not standard in these cases.

Execution Failure

If we are unable to execute and no viable alternative exists: operator fees are returned. Platform planning and oversight fees remain earned once operational coordination has begun.

Unused Credits

After a defined window, partial reimbursement may be requested. Administrative adjustments apply.

IV. MISSION ACTIVATION, PLATFORM FEE & REFUND POLICY

POLICY SPECIFICATIONS
& TERMS

1. ABOUT THE SERVICE

15D Wings provides a digital platform and mission coordination service that connects clients with suitable private aviation supply.

When a client submits a mission request, 15D Wings assesses the requirements, coordinates aircraft sourcing and obtains operator quotations for the proposed flight.

Submitting a mission request does not guarantee aircraft availability or confirm a charter booking.

2. PLATFORM FEE

15D Wings charges a Platform Fee calculated as 10% of the midpoint of the initial mission estimate.

The estimate midpoint is generated by the 15D Wings pricing engine using the applicable pricing methodology and mission requirements.

The Platform Fee is separate from the aircraft operator's charter quotation. The Platform Fee and its calculation will be disclosed to the client before payment.

3. SOURCING ACTIVATION PAYMENT

To activate mission sourcing, the client pays an amount equivalent to 10% of the calculated Platform Fee. This payment is called the Sourcing Activation Payment.

The Sourcing Activation Payment is part of the Platform Fee, not an additional charge.

Where the client accepts the final operator quotation and proceeds with the mission, the activation payment is credited toward the total Platform Fee. The remaining Platform Fee is payable according to the mission payment instructions.

4. WHAT THE ACTIVATION PAYMENT COVERS

The Sourcing Activation Payment activates the agreed mission sourcing and quotation coordination process. This includes:

  • Review and validation of the mission request.
  • Assessment of aircraft requirements.
  • Identification of suitable supply options.
  • Coordination with relevant operators or authorised supply channels.
  • Collection and assessment of operator quotations.
  • Presentation of a recommended quotation and a suitable alternative where useful.

5. IF THE CLIENT DECLINES THE FINAL QUOTATION

If 15D Wings successfully obtains and presents a valid operator quotation that meets the disclosed mission requirements and the client decides not to proceed, the Sourcing Activation Payment becomes the earned sourcing fee.

The activation payment is retained as consideration for the sourcing and quotation coordination service delivered. The client is not required to pay the remaining Platform Fee if the mission does not proceed.

6. IF 15D WINGS CANNOT SOURCE A SUITABLE AIRCRAFT

If 15D Wings cannot obtain a suitable operator quotation for the requested mission, the Sourcing Activation Payment will be refunded. Any deduction for third-party expenses must be separately authorised by the client, documented and genuinely non-refundable.

7. IF THE CLIENT WITHDRAWS BEFORE RECEIVING A QUOTATION

If the client withdraws before 15D Wings delivers a valid operator quotation, the Sourcing Activation Payment will be refunded, subject to separately authorised, documented non-refundable third-party expenses.

8. MATERIAL CHANGES TO THE MISSION

Changes to the route, date, passenger requirements, aircraft category or other material mission specifications may affect the estimate and Platform Fee.

Where a change materially affects the original mission scope, 15D Wings will provide a revised estimate and fee calculation for client approval before proceeding under the revised terms.

9. FINAL OPERATOR QUOTATION

The initial mission estimate is indicative and is not a final charter price. The final operator quotation may vary due to aircraft availability, positioning, fuel, handling, taxes, permits, flight duration and other mission-specific requirements.

15D Wings will communicate material pricing or operational variations before requesting the client's acceptance. The client may accept or decline the final quotation.

10. MISSION CONFIRMATION

A mission is confirmed only after the applicable final quotation has been accepted, required payments have been completed and the relevant operator has confirmed the aircraft and mission arrangements.

Payment of the Sourcing Activation Payment alone does not confirm an aircraft booking.

11. OPERATOR AND OPERATIONAL RESPONSIBILITY

15D Wings coordinates digital mission processing and access to its supply network. The relevant aircraft operator remains responsible for aircraft operation, flight safety, flight planning, weather assessment, NOTAM assessment, alternate planning, regulatory compliance and other operational responsibilities within its remit.

12. REFUNDS

Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment channel where reasonably possible. 15D Wings will communicate the approved amount and expected processing timeline.

Any applicable payment-provider charges, exchange-rate differences or transfer costs that affect the refund will be explained.

13. CLIENT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

Before making payment, the client acknowledges that:

  • The initial estimate is indicative.
  • The Platform Fee is calculated at 10% of the estimate midpoint.
  • The Sourcing Activation Payment is 10% of the Platform Fee.
  • The activation payment is credited toward the Platform Fee if the client proceeds.
  • The activation payment becomes the earned sourcing fee if a valid quotation is delivered and the client declines to proceed.
  • The activation payment is refundable if 15D Wings cannot source a suitable aircraft, subject to authorised and documented non-refundable expenses.
  • Final aircraft availability and charter pricing are subject to operator confirmation.
